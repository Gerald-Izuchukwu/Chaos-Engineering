Welcome to Chaos Engineering

Where systems break on purpose — so they don’t break in production.

This project explores Chaos Engineering: the practice of intentionally injecting failures into systems to understand their weaknesses, improve resilience, and build confidence in production-ready infrastructure.

💥 Controlled Failure

We simulate outages, latency, crashes, and misconfigurations in a controlled environment to observe real system behavior.

🧠 Learn the Unknown

Chaos reveals hidden dependencies, fragile assumptions, and unexpected failure modes you won’t find in diagrams.

🛡️ Build Resilience

The goal isn’t destruction — it’s confidence. Stronger monitoring, better recovery, and systems that survive the unexpected.

☁️ Cloud & Distributed

Designed for modern cloud-native systems: containers, Kubernetes, microservices, load balancers, and real-world traffic.
